## 4. Compression and caching

Configure the safest hosting-supported behavior:

* Brotli and/or Gzip for compressible HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SVG, JSON, XML, and text
* Long-lived immutable caching for content-hashed production assets
* No long-lived caching for HTML documents
* Sensible caching for `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`
* No accidental immutable caching for unhashed files that may change
* Correct MIME types

Preferred behavior:

* Hashed assets: `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`
* HTML: `Cache-Control: no-cache` or an equivalent revalidation policy

Use only settings the current hosting system genuinely supports. If Replit controls compression automatically and the repository cannot configure it, document that and identify the exact production-header checks required after deployment.

Do not claim compression or caching is verified until response headers are checked on the published site.
