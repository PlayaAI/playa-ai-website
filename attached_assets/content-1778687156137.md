§ 02 / What we are building

## Five programs. _One_ _mission._

Not a coalition. Not a manifesto. A working system with shipping artifacts — each one
already in the field, each one publicly governed.

Program 01Reference Implementation

Flagship

### The Oracle

A consent-first AI conversation experience designed to witness, not interpret. The
Oracle does not diagnose, advise, categorize, or decide. It reflects what a participant brings, returns an
artifact in their own language, and asks for consent before anything enters the research commons.

[Request Oracle Brief](https://playaai.github.io/Playa-AI/#)

Program 02Open Protocol

The Standard

### EELP

The Ethical Experience Lab Protocol — an open standard for AI experiences that involve
human stories, consent, community, and trust. AI ethics with receipts.

[Read EELP v1.0](https://playaai.github.io/Playa-AI/#)

Program 03Governance Layer

Trust Architecture

### Data Trust

Participant-controlled governance for stories, consent, deletion, and research access.
No researcher accesses participant data without permission. No story is published without the right consent
layer.

[See Public Use Registry](https://playaai.github.io/Playa-AI/#)

Program 04Replication Package

Experience Lab Kit

### ELK

A replication package for institutions that want to run ethical AI experiences without
reinventing the governance. Space design, facilitation training, consent architecture, compliance checklist.


[Request the Kit](https://playaai.github.io/Playa-AI/#)

Program 05Longitudinal Layer

Layer 0 Community

### Community

The longitudinal research and consent surface before and after the Oracle. Salons,
calls, field activations — where the standard is practiced, not just published.

§ 05 / Why the Playa

## Not a festival. A civic laboratory.

Burning Man is a temporary civilization where gifting, participation, radical
self-expression, decommodification, civic responsibility, and leaving no trace are practiced at scale. Playa
AI uses that environment to test whether AI can be designed around _consent, creativity, trust, and human_
_flourishing_ instead of extraction.

§ 06 / Principles → AI design

## The burner values become a design language.

This is where the cultural thesis becomes practical governance: a translation layer
for teams designing AI experiences around people, memory, consent, and community.

Radical Inclusion

AI that works for everyone.

Gifting

Systems that give value before extracting value.

Decommodification

Technology beyond pure market logic.

Participation

Humans in the loop, deeply engaged.

Radical Self-Expression

AI as creative expansion, not homogenization.

Leaving No Trace

Consent-first data practices. Deletion as standard.

§ 07 / Three paths
