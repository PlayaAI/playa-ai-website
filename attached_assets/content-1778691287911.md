Event grammar

## The rooms are the product.

Across the Luma archive, Playa AI repeatedly uses four formats: intimate salons, hands-on technical sessions, embodied recovery rooms, and civic-cultural launch gatherings.

01

### Curated salons

High-signal circles for agency, trust, identity, and the social consequences of agentic AI.

02

### Working sessions

Mini-hackathons that get people from concept to working agent, with setup and troubleshooting in the room.

03

### Embodied masterminds

Spa, beach, and mountain settings for relaxation, connection, and sharper conversation.

04

### Civic launches

Gatherings that frame Playa AI as a cultural, scientific, and public-interest movement.
