## The Playa AI Council

### Council Charter & Terms of Reference

- ·The Leadership Council is advisory only.
- ·Council members provide perspective, expertise, and strategic input.
- ·Council members have no governance authority.
- ·Council members have no power to bind the foundation.
- ·Council members have no fiduciary duties.
- ·Council members have no signing authority.
- ·Playa AI Foundation has a separate governing board of directors.

Service on the Council does not constitute legal services to Playa AI Foundation. It does not create an attorney-client relationship. It is not privileged. The foundation retains independent counsel for legal matters. Foundation materials should not refer to Rob Griffitts as “counsel,” “general counsel,” or the foundation’s lawyer.

A·001 · Council Member

### Tina _Woods_

Science & Research Lead

Tina is a social entrepreneur, author, and system architect working at the intersection of longevity
science, AI, health policy, and data governance. Founder & CEO of Collider Health and Business for
Health, she builds cross-sector ecosystems focused on preventative health, healthy longevity, and
responsible innovation — across the UK National Innovation Centre for Ageing, the International Institute of
Longevity, the XPRIZE Global Visioneering Brain Trust, and the Global Exposome Forum.

Her book _Live Longer with AI_ explores how artificial intelligence can extend healthspan and
reshape preventative health — directly aligned with Playa AI's focus on human-centered governance. She
co-founded _Longevity Rave_, weaving longevity, music, and intergenerational connection in a way that
resonates naturally with Burning Man culture, and leads the _JoyScore Experiment_, a global
open-science project measuring how human connection drives flourishing — using Playa AI's Connect app as its
technology layer.

At Playa AI, she anchors the science and research dimension — connecting governance frameworks to health,
longevity, data ethics, and the institutional communities needed to turn ideas into real-world adoption.

### Richard _Rothenberg_

Senior AI Advisor

Richard is an AI, quantitative finance, and systemic-risk strategist working at the intersection of big
data, investment research, policy, and sustainable development.

CEO and Co-founder of Global A.I. Corporation, an AI-driven quantitative research company generating
investment and policy insights for governments and institutional investors. His earlier work includes
advising DARPA and the UN on financial vulnerabilities, curating high-profile events in Davos and across the

Richard holds an MBA from Columbia Business School and brings Playa AI a rigorous systems lens on AI,
capital markets, global risk, and institutional decision-making.

### Dr. David _Rock_

NeuroLeadership Lead

Dr. David Rock is a pioneer in applying neuroscience to leadership, culture, motivation, and organizational
change.

Co-founder of the NeuroLeadership Institute, a global consultancy bringing cognitive science into
leadership development, he is widely credited with coining the term _neuroleadership_. His work has
helped move neuroscience into practical use across business, education, coaching, and change management —
including through his books _Your Brain at Work_ and _Coaching with the Brain in Mind_. David
holds a professional doctorate in the Neuroscience of Leadership from Middlesex University.

At Playa AI, he anchors the neuro and human-systems dimension — ensuring the foundation's work around AI,
governance, trust, attention, and collective intelligence is grounded in how humans actually think, relate,
and change.

### Alvin _Wang Graylin_

Senior AI Advisor

Alvin is a global technology strategist, author, and AI pioneer whose work sits at the intersection of
artificial intelligence, spatial computing, and society-scale transformation.

Co-author of _Our Next Reality_, as well as publications on _Abundanism_ and _A Bill of_
